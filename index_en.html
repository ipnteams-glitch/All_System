EA Farming is portfolio management that doesn't rely on a single EA — it spreads risk, similar to how financial institutions manage money. Let's look at A vs B:

A: Diversify with multiple EAs and manage capital per portfolio. The advantage is stability and reduced drawdown, but the cost is complexity from running multiple portfolios.
B: Use a single EA with a clear track record — easy to manage, but high risk when the market changes, like putting all eggs in one basket.

Verdict: When markets are uncertain, A usually wins in the long run because diversification handles risk better. But if you have the skill to pick a single EA that fits market conditions, B can yield high short-term returns. The sweet spot is blending both: diversify with multiple EAs while rebalancing risk for long-term sustainability.
